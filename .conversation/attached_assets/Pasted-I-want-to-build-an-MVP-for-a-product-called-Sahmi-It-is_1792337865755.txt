I want to build an MVP for a product called "Sahmi" (سهمي). It is a new project from scratch.

Problem: University students and people with their first job feel fear and confusion about investing, because they have no way to learn without losing real money.
User: Young men and women aged 18 to 25 in Kuwait. They are beginners at investing, their income is an allowance or a first salary, and they want to start but don't know where.
Solution: We help beginners learn investing through a decision game that uses real historical prices with fake money.
Differentiator: The game rewards smart decisions (spreading the money, staying calm during a drop), not big profit.
- All user-facing text is in Arabic, in simple Kuwaiti dialect, and the UI is right-to-left.
- Every decision is explained in simple words, with no complex terms.
- No real money, so no risk.
- The app is educational only, not investment advice. Show a short disclaimer saying so.

Game concept:
The player manages the money of one fixed character, Fahad, 22, a fresh graduate with his first savings of 1,000 KD and a goal to buy a car in 2 years. Time moves forward month by month through one real historical market period (about 12 months), using real price data. A line chart shows Fahad's balance rising and falling live, and his mood face changes with it (calm, worried, panicking). Time auto-plays and pauses at each decision.

MVP features (only these, do not add anything else):
- One scenario from a real past market (for example the 2020 market drop) with exactly 3 decisions:
  1. Split the 1,000 fake KD across 3 to 4 options (stock fund, bonds, gold, cash).
  2. A market drop happens. The player chooses: hold, sell some, or buy more.
  3. Fahad has a surprise expense of 300 KD. The player chooses where the cash comes from.
- Live balance chart and Fahad's mood face as time plays.
- A short explanation after each decision. The text is pre-written in Arabic and stored with the scenario, not generated.
- A final result screen and a badge based on decision quality, not profit. Score parts: spread (did they diversify), calm (did they avoid panic selling), readiness (did Fahad have cash for the expense). Profit is shown but does not count toward the score.
- A public link (deployment).

Vision (for the future, do NOT build now): more levels and scenarios, AI explanation for each decision, an "what kind of investor are you?" quiz, login and saved progress, challenging friends, live prices, a Shariah-compliant mode.
